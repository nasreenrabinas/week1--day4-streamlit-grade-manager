
# ROLE
Act as an experienced nutrition-focused meal planning assistant with knowledge of traditional South Indian vegetarian cuisine.

# TASK
Create a healthy and balanced 7-day vegetarian South Indian meal plan for gradual weight loss.

# CONTEXT (EDITABLE DETAILS)
Age: 27
Gender: Female
Location: South India
Activity Level: moderate
Health Goal: Gradual and healthy weight loss
Diet Preference: Vegetarian
Allergies: None
Food Dislikes: bitter ground
Cooking Time: 15–30 minutes
People Eating: One adult and one toddler

Change these details whenever you want to generate a different meal plan.

# CONSTRAINTS
- Use traditional South Indian vegetarian dishes.
- Include locally available and affordable ingredients.
- Prefer seasonal vegetables and fruits.
- Include balanced meals with carbohydrates, protein, fibre and healthy fats.
- Use practical and ordinary portion sizes.
- Keep recipes simple and suitable for busy weekdays.
- Avoid extreme calorie restriction and skipping meals.
- Do not include meat, fish or eggs.
- Avoid expensive or unfamiliar ingredients such as quinoa and avocado.
- Respect all allergies and food dislikes mentioned in the CONTEXT section.
- Do not assume any medical conditions.
- Do not give the toddler an adult weight-loss diet. Mention that toddler portions and nutritional needs are different.

# PLAN
Prepare a complete 7-day meal plan from Monday to Sunday.

Include four meals every day:
1. Breakfast
2. Lunch
3. Evening Snack
4. Dinner

Use different South Indian dishes throughout the week and avoid unnecessary repetition.

# FORMAT
Present the meal plan in one clear table.

Use these four columns:

| Day | Meal | Dish | Portion |

Requirements:
- Include all seven days.
- Include all four meals for each day.
- Mention specific dish names instead of vague descriptions.
- Give portion sizes using cups, pieces or grams.
- Keep the table simple and easy to read.

# AVOID
- Repeating the same meals every day.
- Complicated recipes requiring too much preparation.
- Expensive imported ingredients.
- Extreme dieting and unrealistic food restrictions.
- Missing meals or portion sizes.
- Ignoring allergies or food dislikes.

# RESPONSE
- Provide a clear, practical and easy-to-follow meal plan.
- Use simple English.
- Briefly explain any important portion-size recommendations.
- Check that all seven days and four meals are included before providing the final answer.
- Present the final answer only in the requested table format, followed by brief practical notes.

Note: This is a general meal-planning draft for educational purposes, not personalised medical or dietitian advice.
